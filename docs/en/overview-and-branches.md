# Imperial Barbershops - Overview and Branch Locations

The **Imperial Barbershop Chain** is a premier grooming network that unites vintage barber traditions with modern aesthetics and personal wellness. Founded in 2018, the network operates 4 strategic branches, each tailored to distinct client profiles and lifestyle needs.

---

## Our 4 Branches

### 1. Downtown Branch (Heritage Flagship)

The original branch, inspired by 1950s classic barbershops featuring original cast-iron chairs and checkerboard tiling.

- **Address:** 142 Flower Street - Downtown
- **Opening Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 09:00 AM to 08:00 PM
- **Key Features:**
  - Traditional straight-razor shaving with hot eucalyptus-infused towels.
  - Complimentary craft draft beer and espresso for seated clients.
  - Vintage pool table and retro lounge music.

### 2. Jardins Branch (Executive & Spa)

Crafted specifically for business executives and clients who demand privacy, refinement, and comprehensive relaxation.

- **Address:** 1500 Paulista Avenue - Jardins District
- **Opening Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday, 08:00 AM to 09:00 PM; Sunday, 10:00 AM to 04:00 PM
- **Key Features:**
  - Private executive suites with high-speed Wi-Fi (take conference calls during your service).
  - Men's facial spa, anti-stress scalp massages, and podiatry care.
  - Curated single-malt whiskey and specialty coffee menu.

### 3. Shopping Plaza Branch (Express)

Designed for speed, convenience, and busy families on the go.

- **Address:** Plaza South Mall, Level 2 - Suite 244
- **Opening Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Sundays and Holidays, 02:00 PM to 08:00 PM
- **Key Features:**
  - Walk-in friendly with minimal wait times; appointments optional.
  - Kids grooming station with gaming consoles and themed novelty chairs.
  - Direct parking and food court connectivity.

### 4. Studio Femme Branch (Exclusively for Women)

A unique concept studio, **100% dedicated to female clients**, created for women seeking contemporary short cuts, visagism, and specialized hair care in an intimate, relaxing space.

- **Address:** 88 Acacias Boulevard - Vila Nova
- **Opening Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday, 09:00 AM to 07:00 PM
- **Target Audience:** Exclusively female (no male services conducted here).
- **Key Features:**
  - Specialized in modern female short haircuts (Pixie cuts, French bobs, Long bobs, and feminine undercuts).
  - Facial visagism consultations and precision eyebrow mapping/design.
  - Deep hydration therapies, customized capillary schedules, and premium coloring.
  - Zen lounge with herbal teas, sparkling wine, and ambient acoustic music.
