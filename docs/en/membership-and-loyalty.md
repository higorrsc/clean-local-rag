# Imperial Barbershops - Membership Club and Loyalty Program

The **Imperial Club** is our monthly recurring membership program, engineered to deliver convenience, cost savings, and VIP benefits across all 4 locations.

---

## Monthly Membership Tiers

### Silver Tier (Essential Grooming)

- **Monthly Fee:** $59.00 / month
- **Included Benefits:**
  - 2 Haircuts per month (valid at Downtown, Jardins, or Shopping Plaza).
  - 10% discount on all home-care grooming products (shampoos, oils, balms).
  - 1 complimentary draft beer or specialty espresso per visit.

### Gold Tier (Complete Style)

- **Monthly Fee:** $99.00 / month
- **Included Benefits:**
  - Unlimited Haircuts throughout the month.
  - 2 Complete Imperial Beard Therapies per month.
  - 15% discount on all single services and treatments.
  - Priority booking status in the mobile app.

### Diamond & Family Tier (Cross-Branch VIP)

- **Monthly Fee:** $149.00 / month
- **Included Benefits:**
  - Unlimited Haircuts and Beard Grooming across all men's branches.
  - Complimentary access to private executive suites at the Jardins branch.
  - **Studio Femme Cross-Benefit:** The membership holder can transfer 1 complimentary female haircut or deep conditioning session monthly to their partner, mother, or daughter at Studio Femme.
  - 20% discount on all cosmetics and premium styling products.

---

## Rewards & Loyalty Points

- **Points per Dollar:** Earn 1 Imperial Point for every $1.00 spent on retail products or non-included services.
- **Redemption Options:**
  - **150 points:** 1 Premium styling pomade or beard butter.
  - **300 points:** 1 Free beard grooming or manicure session.
  - **500 points:** 1 Free signature haircut (men's traditional or Studio Femme visagism cut).
- **Validity:** Loyalty points remain active for 12 months from the date they are earned.
