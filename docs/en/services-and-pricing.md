## 3. Booking and Punctuality Policies

- **Reservations:** Bookings can be made via the Imperial Barbershop Mobile App or by phone. Shopping Plaza branch welcomes walk-ins.
- **Grace Period (Late Arrival):** We extend a **15-minute grace period** for appointments. Arrivals later than 15 minutes may be subject to rescheduling based on barber availability.
- **Cancellation Policy:** Cancellations made at least **2 hours in advance** incur no cancellation fee.
- **Accepted Payments:** All major credit/debit cards, contactless mobile pay, and cash. Club members receive automated monthly billing.
